SAGND FINAL RESULTS INTERPRETATION
==================================

Benchmark input
---------------
The independently reconstructed benchmark contained 3,695 cells
and 16,324 genes.

Global expression modification
------------------------------
SAGND produced a mean absolute expression change of
0.066343. The corresponding values were
0.147260 for KNN and
0.073630 for the GraphSmooth-style baseline.

Structure preservation
----------------------
SAGND preserved cell-level expression structure with a Spearman
correlation of 0.972092. Gene-level mean
expression preservation was 0.998908.

Specificity-aware behavior
--------------------------
The correlation between gene specificity and absolute expression
change under SAGND was -0.845658.

Low-specificity genes showed a mean absolute change of
0.206724, whereas
high-specificity genes showed a mean absolute change of
0.000342.

The high-to-low specificity change ratio was
0.001654.

Marker preservation
-------------------
Across the seven-marker panel, SAGND achieved a mean marker-level
Spearman correlation of 0.758984 and a mean Pearson
correlation of 0.974058.

The mean top-10% marker-cell Jaccard overlap was
0.803868.

Cluster structure
-----------------
The Stage34 method-specific benchmark recorded
14 clusters and a silhouette score of
0.433087 for SAGND.

Benchmark interpretation
------------------------
Together, these results are consistent with the intended behavior of
SAGND: expression modification is concentrated more strongly among
lower-specificity genes while high-specificity genes undergo much
smaller changes, and broad cell-level and marker-level structure
remains strongly preserved.

Important benchmark limitations
--------------------------------
MAGIC was not executed because of environment/package incompatibility.
Therefore no numerical MAGIC comparison is reported.

The GraphSmooth-style comparator is a simplified propagation baseline
and is not claimed to reproduce an exact historical GraphSmooth
implementation.

The historical notebook contains a 2,960-cell × 2,000-gene analysis
state, but the exact cell-level identity of that historical subset
could not be independently reconstructed. The Stage34 benchmark
therefore uses the independently reconstructed 3,695 × 16,324 input
and did not force a 2,960-cell selection.

No MFOS or CELLDEC data were used.
